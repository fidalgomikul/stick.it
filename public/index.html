    Print guides & framing

    Keep faces and text inside the dashed blue line — trimming and a mat can hide a few millimetres of each edge.

    Extra background added around the edges of the export and cut off after printing, so no white slivers show at the edges. Ask your print shop; 3 mm is typical.

    The mat and frame are only a preview; they're never in the exported file.

    Random layout

    This style sizes stickers automatically to fill the space; each sticker's Size weight still applies.

    Locked items (lock icon in Layers) always stay where they are. Scatter, Pile, Packed and Shape place new stickers around them; Grid and Circle ignore them.
